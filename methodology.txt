Methodology for Your Paper

To prevent data leakage, the HAM10000 dataset was partitioned at the lesion level rather than the image level. All images corresponding to the same lesion identifier (lesion_id) were assigned exclusively to either the training or validation set. An 80:20 lesion-wise split was performed using stratified sampling based on the diagnostic class (dx) with a fixed random seed of 42 to ensure reproducibility. This strategy preserved the class distribution while preventing images from the same lesion from appearing in both subsets.
